HOW TO ADD YOUR PHOTOS
======================

1. Put as many photos as you like in this "photos" folder (5, 20, 50 — any number).
2. Name them in order:  photo1.jpg, photo2.jpg, photo3.jpg ... photo20.jpg
   (.jpg, .jpeg, .png and .webp all work)
3. Refresh the page. The slideshow shows every photo automatically, in number order.

Lazy option (Mac): put your photos here with ANY names, then double-click
"rename-photos.command". It renames them all to photo1, photo2, photo3 ... for you.
(If macOS blocks it the first time: right-click it → Open.)

Note: iPhone .HEIC photos don't show in browsers. Export them as JPG first
(Photos app → File → Export), or AirDrop them to the Mac as "Most Compatible".

Captions and slide speed: open index.html, find "CONFIG" near the bottom,
and edit "captions" and "slideSeconds".
